The Governance Problem

As populations grow, manual grievance handling fails to scale. Fragmented communication, unorganized data, and manual sorting cause critical bottlenecks:

  • Delayed complaint resolution times causing public dissatisfaction
  • Lack of real-time tracking transparency for citizens
  • No centralized data analytics to spot recurring local issues

The Tech-Driven Solution

Our platform automates intake, structures unstructured data, and intelligently routes tasks directly to relevant administrative units:

  • Automated Sorting: Instant AI tagging and ticket prioritization
  • Live Lifecycle Tracking: Transparent updates for citizens & officers
  • Constituency Analytics: Automated heatmap insights for intervention

Meet MAULI

Management & Automation Unit for Local Issues

Constituency-Scale AI Governance Operating System

MAULI is a dedicated AI software suite engineered to manage vast administrative workloads with corporate headquarters efficiency. It collects citizen inputs, extracts issue categories, structures workloads, and generates real-time actionable intelligence.

By transforming raw public complaints into structured data points, MAULI empowers public representatives and decision-makers to handle mass grievances proactively rather than reactively.

Mass Redressal

Processes thousands of incoming citizen inputs effortlessly, unifying WhatsApp, web forms, and field notes into structured records.

Intelligent Routing

Uses Natural Language Processing to direct tickets directly to relevant departments without manual triage delays.

Data Intelligence

Generates real-time analytical dashboards showing local issue hotspots, pendency rates, and resolution efficiency.
