The Problem with Traditional Systems

Governance is only as strong as its ability to listen—and respond. An effective system ensures that every citizen’s voice is not only heard, but acknowledged and acted upon with speed and accountability.

As grievance volumes increase and issues become more complex, traditional manual systems struggle to keep pace—leading to delays, inefficiencies, and loss of public trust.

A Smarter Approach

Our AI-powered grievance redressal platform bridges this gap by combining intelligent automation with structured workflows.

  • Instant categorization of complaints
  • Automated routing to relevant departments
  • Real-time tracking and status visibility
  • Data-driven insights for decision making

This is not just digitization. It is a shift towards responsive, accountable, and citizen-first governance.

MAULI – Grievance Intelligence System

MAULI is a unified platform designed to streamline communication between citizens and administrative systems. It enables structured grievance handling, real-time monitoring, and data-backed governance decisions.

  • Citizen-first submission (WhatsApp & forms)
  • AI-powered summarization and categorization
  • Automated routing workflows
  • Live dashboards and analytics

From Complaints to Intelligence

Every grievance is not just a problem—it is data. Our platform transforms raw inputs into actionable insights, helping institutions identify patterns, prioritize interventions, and improve governance outcomes at scale.
